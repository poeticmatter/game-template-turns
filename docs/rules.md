# Pig: how to play

*The rules as the game plays them now, in plain language. Pig is the template's example game: replace this file with your own game's rules.*

Pig is a push-your-luck dice game for 2 to 5 players. Roll as often as you dare, but a 1 wipes out everything you rolled that turn. The first to reach the target score wins.

## What's on the table

- One six-sided die.
- A score for each player, starting at 0.
- A target score, chosen when the game is created: 20, 50 (the default) or 100.

## Your turn

Players take turns in seat order. On your turn, do either of these as often as you like:

- **Roll** the die. A 2 to 6 adds to your **turn total**. A **1** wipes out your turn total and ends your turn.
- **Hold** (once you've rolled at least once): add your turn total to your score and end your turn.

## End of the game

As soon as a player holds and their score reaches the target, they win.
